# Code of Conduct

MinimalSF is a small project run in spare time. To keep it a friendly place:

- **Be kind and respectful**, to the maintainer and to each other. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- **No harassment, insults, or discrimination** of any kind, including about age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience, nationality, religion or sexual orientation.
- **No spam or self-promotion** in issues and pull requests.
- **Accept a "no" gracefully.** Not every idea fits a minimal launcher, and that's not a judgement of you.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules.
To report something, mention @DHIRAJ-J-S, or for anything that can't be public, open a [private report](https://github.com/DHIRAJ-J-S/MinimalSF_Launcher/security/advisories/new).
