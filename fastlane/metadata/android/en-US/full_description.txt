MinimalSF is a minimal launcher built for one thing: speed. Type a letter or two and your app is already open, often before you finish the name. No icon hunting, no clutter, just a black &amp; white home screen that gets out of your way. The whole app is under 1 MB.

<b>Type to launch</b><ul><li>Keyboard is ready the moment you're home</li><li>One match opens by itself, with a delay you set (down to 0ms)</li><li>Several matches are numbered: type w2 to open the 2nd result for w</li><li>Enter opens the top result; results are ranked by best match</li><li>Custom keywords per app (wa for WhatsApp), optionally replacing the app's name in search, and hide apps from search</li></ul><b>Gestures</b><ul><li>Double tap, long press and swipes in four directions</li><li>Open an app (search for it by name), call a contact, run a shortcut or open a specific app screen</li><li>Flashlight, camera, music controls, lock screen, notifications, quick settings</li><li>Saves "add to home screen" shortcuts from other apps, including Activity Launcher</li></ul><b>Home screen</b><ul><li>Show only what you want: hide the clock, date, screen time, music bar, todo list or buttons</li><li>Large clock (tap for alarms) and date, with optional screen time and RAM use</li><li>Optional now playing bar: tap to play/pause, swipe to skip</li><li>Inline todo list: mark tasks important, undo a removal, long press to edit</li><li>Landscape layout for full and floating keyboards</li><li>All apps as a list, a list without icons, or a grid, with an optional A-Z scrubber; includes work profile and dual apps</li></ul><b>Make it yours</b><ul><li>Fonts: monospace, clean, your phone's font, free Google Fonts, or import a .ttf/.otf, plus bold</li><li>Font and clock size, 12/24h clock, search bar at the top or bottom</li></ul><b>Want more?</b><ul><li>The optional <b>MinimalSF Extended</b> plugin adds open in freeform, force stop, clear cache or data and freeze to the long press menu (through Shizuku or root)</li><li>It also brings extras that stay out of the launcher itself: coloured icons, a hidden search bar, turning off auto-launch, and choosing what the long press menu shows</li><li>Without it, MinimalSF stays exactly as it is</li></ul><b>Private</b><ul><li>No internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no tracking</li><li>Everything stays on your device</li></ul>Optional features need permissions you grant yourself: accessibility (only to lock the screen), notification access (now playing bar) and usage access (screen time). Font downloads use Google Play services when it's installed.
